The MediPulse Clinical Supply Loop

How intelligent batch scanning and statistical algorithms prevent medication stockouts and eliminate capital write-offs.

1

GS1-128 Inbound Docking

Each inbound medication and implant lot is barcode-scanned at hospital receiving dock with lot number, manufacture date, and verified expiration.

2

Cold-Chain & FEFO Sorting

Storage temperatures are digitally logged. Batches are cataloged strictly by First-Expire-First-Out priority for automatic ward allocation.

3

Statistical ROP Buffer

The engine evaluates ROP = (d × L) + SS. When burn rate surges in Trauma OT or ICU, purchase orders trigger before stock hits safety buffers.

4

Automated Ward Swaps

Near-expiry batches (< 60D) detected in low-velocity departments are automatically recommended for transfer to high-burn Emergency OT, saving thousands in waste.
